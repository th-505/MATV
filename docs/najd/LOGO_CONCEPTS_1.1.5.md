# Four original Najd mark concepts

1. **بوابة نجد** — a smooth Najdi doorway combined with a dune/play opening and a small four-point star. The current original master `assets/brand/najd-mark.png` implements this direction. The symbol remains readable without tiny lettering. Square app icons use a near-black background and lavender symbol; TV uses the same symbol in an adaptive safe region.
2. **نجد بالكلمة** — a custom flowing Arabic «نجد» wordmark with a triangular negative-space play cut. Avoid detailed calligraphy at launcher sizes; use a simplified initial stroke as the app icon.
3. **الأفق** — two continuous dune curves inside an open circular horizon, with a small lavender sunrise/play accent. The outer shape remains clear on circular TV launchers and square mobile icons.
4. **نجم الديرة** — an original four-point star whose inner negative space suggests a play button, paired with the Arabic/English product name. No airline silhouette, borrowed logo or pixel grid.

Palette reference: Riyadh Air official lavender values in `shared/najd-brand.json`; the airline is a color reference only. Use one selected master across platforms, never four competing production identities. Native assets are rendered from the master with Lanczos resampling and adequate padding, not bitmap enlargement from a launcher thumbnail.
